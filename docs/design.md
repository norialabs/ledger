# Accounting design

How the engine is built and which invariants it enforces. What is still missing is in [roadmap.md](roadmap.md).

## Ownership

The financial boundary is tenant → legal entity → book → journal → posting. A book represents an accounting treatment and functional currency. Branches, projects, departments, campuses, and funds are dimensions rather than separate legal entities unless their legal/accounting requirements warrant separate entities.

A consulting firm accesses a client's tenant through host-managed delegations. It does not own all client books in a shared accounting tenant. The firm can also have its own tenant for its own accounts. A client can use several firms or change firms while keeping stable tenant/entity/book IDs and history. The actor and acting firm are recorded separately.

The engine owns financial postings, their source snapshots, open items, allocation and revaluation history, recognition schedules, stock valuation, asset financial registers, bank evidence, and derived financial reports. Host applications own their operational records (customers, products, warehouses, users, subscriptions, approvals). References connect these domains without putting their records inside ledger tables.

## Implemented invariants

1. A journal's functional debits equal its functional credits exactly. Positive source amounts are retained even when quantization produces zero functional units.
2. Posting rows, open-item identities, audit records, and batch memberships derive from their parent events. They cannot be independently changed or deleted through ordinary DML.
3. Source references and retry keys have separate uniqueness guarantees. A retry cannot change content.
4. A journal and every derived record commit atomically. Failed work does not consume a book sequence.
5. Accounts, parties, dimensions, journals, and allocations cannot cross their tenant/book/entity boundaries.
6. Period closing and posting contend on the same book lock. Each new posting must have a covering open period.
7. Posted history changes through compensating events. Reversal journals exactly invert the original postings, including currencies, rates, party, and dimensions.
8. Allocation cannot exceed either remaining original amount or incorrectly consume its functional carrying value. Releases restore their exact allocation and linked FX treatment.
9. An original journal with active allocations or bank matches cannot be reversed until those links are released.
10. A control-account valuation must be linked to a corresponding settlement or revaluation before commit. A closing journal must belong to a fiscal closure. Registered inventory, asset, and monetary valuations require their corresponding events. Checks run after journal projections and cannot be bypassed by switching constraints to immediate mode or wrapping commands in an outer transaction.
11. Bank matching changes reconciliation evidence, not cash balances. Statements must independently reconcile to their closing totals.
12. Report numerical results are bounded by both accounting date and committed book sequence. Backdating cannot alter a saved numerical snapshot.
13. Application policies can add restrictions but cannot relax these storage checks.
14. Inventory issues cannot overdraw quantity or cost. FIFO layer allocations and value adjustments conserve every minor unit; reversals restore the original layers in dependency order.
15. Asset plans conserve remaining depreciable value. Events use the active plan, preserve residual value, and cannot silently rewrite earlier history. Disposal recognizes due and convention-based partial-period depreciation before calculating gain/loss.
16. Group reports retain explicit source cutoffs, mappings, rates/historical equity amounts, elimination cutoffs, policy, and immutable JSONB results. Source permissions are rechecked when reading a saved report.
17. Stock returns cannot exceed the original issue's remaining quantity or original cost. Cross-pool returns preserve original counter-account dimensions. Transfer movements conserve both quantity and cost, commit together, and can only be corrected together.
18. Foreign monetary eligibility is explicit. Revaluations retain original currency balances, handle signed/zero-source balances, and classify cash adjustments as exchange effects. Subsequent postings cannot silently backdate before a recorded valuation in the same dimension bucket.
19. Configuration versions retain immutable JSONB definitions, normalized-storage hashes, canonical content hashes, and book-owned role mappings. Reinstallation cannot change a version; new versions leave earlier evidence intact. Treatments and tax documents record the explicitly selected configuration and exact inputs.
20. Supplier returns conserve original receipt-price credits separately from stock carrying value, with explicit variance postings. FIFO returns cannot consume an unrelated receipt layer. Corrections restore quantity, layers, commercial credit, and variance together.
21. Capitalized additions and proportionate partial disposals retain gross-cost effects, accumulated depreciation/impairment effects, and replacement plans. PostgreSQL independently verifies the event and conserving plan; corrections restore all original effects and the previous plan.
22. Saved declarative statements retain ordered definitions, comparative cutoffs, dimension filters, and hash-checked JSONB results. Formula cycles and requested-coverage omissions fail explicitly.
23. Book integration cursors advance in commit-serialized book order, independently of global audit IDs. Event indexes and audit payloads commit with the command; ordinary DML cannot fabricate or rewrite them.
24. Every table begins with its sole primary key `id`: UUIDv7 defaults on 57 tables and ordered bigint identity on audit history. Imported UUID versions remain accepted. Scoped ownership/natural/relationship keys remain unique alternate keys, with composite foreign keys enforcing isolation. All tables retain creation timestamps; mutable catalog/period rows retain database-managed modification timestamps.
25. Catalog soft deletion retires identities without filtering historical financial results. New activity validates live references under database locks, including zero-value valuation events. Exact corrections preserve historical references and no financial/evidence record is soft deleted.
26. Registered finance contracts pin initial configuration, full dimension bucket and payment/obligation inputs. PostgreSQL independently verifies funding, ordered period effects, ECL changes, credit-impaired net interest, write-off/recovery limits and cumulative revenue allocations. Account-only journals and detached corrections fail at commit, including attempts to escape through a different dimension bucket.
27. Tax claims conserve cumulative eligibility across periods; exact source reversals recapture earlier claimed fractions. Prepared returns retain configuration, source/decision cutoffs and hash-checked results. PostgreSQL independently recomputes results and requires review/mapping/posting reconciliation before finalization. Amendments preserve their original chain, and opening credits reference the current filed preceding version.
28. Statutory payload bytes, source hashes, route, environment, adapter version and external retry identity are immutable. One current worker claim controls each delivery; a superseding claim fences old outcomes. Ambiguous transmission and expired leases require lookup, not a blind resend. Accepted invoices anchor bounded original-currency credits, and transport outcomes never post accounting settlement.

The tests exercise these through both public commands and direct SQL under a role subject to RLS. Concurrent posting, allocation, stock issue/return/transfer, depreciation, and monetary-valuation tests use separate processes. This establishes the tested behaviors, not an external accounting audit or a throughput certification.

Tenant policies compare UUID columns directly with the UUID-valued session setting. Casting every indexed tenant column to text adds per-row work and misleading selectivity to scoped report joins. Empty scope reads no rows; malformed UUID scope fails. Open items copy their posting's party, effective date and book sequence, which the posting trigger writes and nothing else can. Pages and party reads therefore walk an index in report order and compute each item's outstanding amount from its own allocations and revaluations. Whole-book aging, exposure and reconciliation aggregate those events once per cutoff and join the results instead. Party balances are driven from the party index, summing each party's postings on the account. Every paged or per-party read costs the same whatever the size of the book.

A book's sequence counter lives on its row. Updating one row thousands of times in a transaction leaves a version chain that every later lookup of the book in that transaction walks, so a billing run that bumped the counter per journal was quadratic. `postRun()` instead reserves each chunk's range once in `ledger_sequence_reservations`, and the journal stamp accepts an explicit sequence only inside a reservation made by the current transaction. The reservation row is evidence of who reserved which range. PostgreSQL needs planner statistics before a first large run; without them every `(tenant_id, book_id, …)` index looks equally selective and foreign-key checks can scan the book. Local scale and recovery evidence is recorded in the roadmap.

## Fresh schema layout

Nine ordered Laravel migrations group core, inventory, assets, group accounting, currency valuation, finance, tax, statutory delivery and cross-domain integrity. The first eight use `Schema::create()` for all 59 tables, ordinary indexes, alternate keys, composite ownership foreign keys and lifecycle columns, and install their domain-specific database rules. The core owns shared lifecycle functions; the final migration attaches cross-domain journal/settlement links and protected-register account guards after every domain exists. `Storage\PostgresSchema` shares UUIDv7/audit identities, six-digit timezone-aware timestamps, evidence columns, lifecycle triggers and SQL installation/cleanup. Circular inventory links use Laravel's deferred foreign-key API after their related tables exist. Unconstrained `numeric` uses `rawColumn()` so explicit precision checks reject invalid values before coercion.

Ten SQL files retain PostgreSQL-specific checks, RLS policies, triggers, functions and partial/expression indexes by accounting area, including shared storage and cross-domain integrity. The shared loader preserves quoted strings and dollar-quoted function bodies while executing statements through Laravel's connection, so query logs and dry runs include database rules. Laravel tracks and transacts each migration separately. Rollback removes cross-domain wiring first, then each domain's functions and tables, releasing circular inventory foreign keys before dropping related tables; core/shared functions are removed last. Migration acceptance covers full and one-step rollback/reinstallation, retained core evidence and domain protections at the integrity boundary, dry runs, selected-schema isolation, all first-column/single-primary-key contracts, timestamp precision, v7 defaults, alternate relationship keys, deferred/scoped foreign keys, RLS and enabled triggers. All nine form the required baseline. Until the first release the baseline is edited in place; after it, schema changes use incremental Laravel migrations.

Trigger names follow `stage_<two-digit number>_<purpose>`. Stages 10–21 enforce scope, immutability, derived-row access and finance-account guards; 30–40 stamp and validate evidence; 60–70 enforce catalog lifecycle and timestamps; 80 projects derived rows; 90 records audit evidence; 99 checks linked accounting integrity. Within matching timing, level and event, PostgreSQL sorts triggers alphabetically, including purpose suffixes within a stage. Constraint triggers explicitly declare `DEFERRABLE INITIALLY DEFERRED`; the stage number preserves ordering when constraints are checked immediately.

## Host deployment

The engine shares the host application's database connection, schema and transaction, so a host can write its operational record and the journal that accounts for it atomically, or post later from its own outbox. Its tables carry the `ledger_` prefix and its tenant isolation uses its own session setting, `noria.ledger_tenant`, beside the host's own RLS settings; a host uses its workspace or school UUID as the ledger tenant. All nine migrations install together: the integrity migration links every domain, so unused domains cost empty tables rather than optional, separately tested schemas.

## Extension boundaries

The Laravel container resolves each service with explicit scoped dependencies. Host readonly subclasses can extend application orchestration. Versioned `PostingTreatment` implementations generate journals using immutable configuration roles; `PostingPolicy` implementations can add validation. Arithmetic, balancing, ownership, chronology, and subledger linkage remain database/core contracts. Configuration installation and runtime extension registration do not permit arbitrary SQL valuation algorithms or change posted history.

Configuration packs are reusable definitions rather than approved country policy. Installation validates chart dependencies and immutable account classifications, binds posting roles to the selected book, and retains exact definitions. Taxed documents select ordered effective-dated rules and retain their calculations. Statement definitions use account selectors and a restricted exact formula graph; explicitly ordered rows survive canonical serialization.

Reporting policy belongs in versioned mappings and statement definitions, separately from fundamental account types and cash-flow classifications. IFRS 18 profit-or-loss categories must not inherit cash-flow categories; classification can depend on the entity's main business activities. OCI, equity movement attribution, MPM reconciliations and retrospective comparatives need explicit evidence contracts before public report types are finalized. These are pending capabilities in the roadmap, not guarantees of the current generic statement renderer. Hosts own policy judgments, eligibility decisions and narrative disclosures.
